Exitails Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Exitails ("the game") is a free puzzle game for Android published by Arda Yılmaz ("we"). This page explains what information is handled when you play.

The short version

  • The game has no accounts and asks for no name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your progress (levels, stars, chosen theme and character, settings) is stored only on your device and is never sent to us.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google collects some device information to show and measure those ads.

Information collected by advertising

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). When ads are requested or shown, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID;
  • IP address, from which an approximate location (country or city) can be derived;
  • information about the ads you see and tap, and about how the game performs (for example diagnostics from the ads component).

Google uses this to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance and prevent fraud. Depending on your region and your choices, ads may be personalised. How Google uses this data is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

We do not receive your advertising ID or IP address, and we do not combine advertising data with anything else.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): when the game first starts, you are asked whether you agree to personalised ads. To be asked again, clear the game's storage in Android Settings → Apps → Exitails → Storage.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Deleting your data: uninstalling the game or clearing its storage removes everything it keeps on your device. There is nothing stored on our side to delete.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Purchases

If the game offers a subscription, payment is handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
